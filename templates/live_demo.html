Active Feed
Active Zone Constraint: The yellow bounding box in the video feed represents the Interaction Area. Hand tracking only updates the mouse cursor position when your pointer finger is inside this zone.

Gesture Action Guide

Hover (Cursor Navigation)
Extend index finger only. Moves cursor smoothly.
Left Click
Pinch thumb and index. Triggers mouse click.
Position Scroll (Index + Pinky)
Above half-screen: Scroll UP. Below half-screen: Scroll DOWN.
Brightness Rotation (5 Fingers)
Rotate open hand Clockwise: Brightness UP. Anti-clockwise: Brightness DOWN.
Capture Screenshot
Extend thumb, index, and pinky. Saves image with flash.
Minimize Window
Extend index, middle, and pinky. Minimizes current window.
Maximize Window
Extend index, middle, ring, pinky. Maximizes current window.
Close Window
Peace / V-Sign (Index + Middle extended). Sends Alt+F4 command.
Right Click
Pinch thumb and middle finger. Triggers right click.
Volume Control (Thumb Up / Down)
Thumb UP: Volume Up. Thumb DOWN: Volume Down.
Mute / Unmute Volume
Extend little finger (pinky) only. Toggles master audio mute.
Track Navigation (Pointing Left / Right)
Point RIGHT: Previous Track. Point LEFT: Next Track.
Pause Video
Make a Fist on RIGHT side of frame. Sends Space key.
Play Video
Make a Fist on LEFT side of frame. Sends Space key.
Save File
Cross Index & Middle fingers. Sends Ctrl+S command.
Camera Zoom In / Out
Extend 3 fingers (Index + Middle + Ring). Move TOWARDS camera: Zoom In (Ctrl++). Move AWAY: Zoom Out (Ctrl+-).

Developer Tips

  • Stabilization: The HUD draws real-time progress bars for each command. Keep a gesture held until the stabilizer lock fills up to trigger the action.
  • Calibration: Face the camera directly. Adequate lighting around your hand improves MediaPipe's joint detection precision.
  • Safety: Do not run this program on headless or shared servers, as anyone who connects can control your machine's inputs.
